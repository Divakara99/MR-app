Privacy Policy — MR Vacancies Hub

Last updated: Oct 19, 2025 • Managed by M Divakar Reddy

MR Vacancies Hub (“we”, “app”) helps Medical Representatives find verified pharma vacancies. This policy describes how we handle data for Play Store Data Safety.

Data Collection

  • Contact info (name, phone, email) — you provide when applying.
  • App activity (jobs viewed, saved, applied) — for recommendations & analytics.
  • Device / Ads ID (AdMob) — for showing personalized / non-personalized ads.

How we use data

  • To show verified vacancies and masked contacts, process unlocks via ads/subscriptions.
  • For AdMob ads: banner, interstitial, rewarded, native. You can opt-out of personalized ads in Google Settings.
  • We do NOT sell data. Recruiter contacts are masked to prevent spam.

Data sharing & encryption

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. No sharing with third parties except Google AdMob & Play Billing (subscriptions) and Gemini API (AI assistant, if enabled).

Data deletion & opt-out

Email support@mrvacancieshub.in to delete your account/data. Ads opt-out: device Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Opt-out.

Children

App is for 18+ jobseekers. Content rating: Everyone. No known child-directed content.

Permissions

PWA requests: notifications (job alerts) optional; no location/background tracking without consent.
