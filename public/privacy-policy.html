Privacy Policy — MR Vacancies Hub

Effective from: 20 October 2026 • Since 2026 • Managed by M Divakar Reddy, App Founder

The complete Privacy Policy and Terms & Privacy are also available inside the MR Vacancies Hub app under Legal & Support. For help, raise a support ticket from AI Assistant → Raise Support Ticket or email akhildivakara@gmail.com.

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your full name, 10-digit mobile number, correct email ID and selected role (Job Seeker or Recruiter). You stay logged in on your device until you choose Logout. After logout, your mobile number is used to find your existing account.

Job Seeker profile information

Job Seekers provide job-related details such as Fresher or Experienced status, qualification, passing year for Freshers, desired role, experience, current company and role, current and expected package, State, City, HQ, joining availability, two-wheeler availability and skills.

Registered Recruiters can view these job-fit details under an anonymous candidate code. A Job Seeker's name, mobile number and email ID are not shown to Recruiters. A Recruiter may request contact through Admin, and direct contact details should be shared only after Admin review and candidate consent.

Recruiter and vacancy information

Recruiters provide account and vacancy details, including company, role, HQ, CTC, qualification and contact information. Vacancies are reviewed by Admin M Divakar Reddy before they go LIVE. Recruiter contact details may be masked until they are revealed under the applicable plan or sponsored-ad unlock rules.

How information is used

  • To operate Job Seeker and Recruiter accounts and maintain a persistent session until logout.
  • To show relevant vacancies and anonymous candidate profiles.
  • To process applications, saved jobs, subscriptions, contact reveals and candidate contact requests.
  • To review recruiter-submitted vacancies and Job Seeker account-deletion requests.
  • To respond to AI Assistant questions, support emails and support tickets.
  • To prevent spam, fraud, misuse and unauthorized account activity.
  • To operate plan-based sponsored ads, including the Free-plan 3 daily unlocks, 7 extra jobs with separate detail-reveal ads, and the Apply Now sponsored ad shown on Free, Starter and Pro plans.

AI Assistant and support tickets

Questions entered into the AI Assistant are processed to generate responses. When you raise a support ticket, we collect your name, mobile number, email ID, selected proper reason, subject and detailed issue explanation so Admin can investigate and respond. Do not enter passwords, OTPs, bank details, Aadhaar/PAN numbers or other highly sensitive information into AI Chat or a support ticket.

Support email: akhildivakara@gmail.com. Please mention your registered mobile number and ticket number for faster help.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Anonymous Job Seeker job-fit details may be shown to Recruiters as described above. Information may also be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as hosting, database, email, advertising, analytics, security and AI-assistant services, or when required for safety or by applicable law.

Storage, retention and security

Account information is retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period needed for support, safety, disputes and legal or operational records. Support-ticket history may be retained after resolution. We use reasonable safeguards and privacy masking, but no internet system can be guaranteed completely secure. Log out on shared devices.

Your choices and account deletion

Job Seekers can update their profile in the app. Recruiters can directly delete their Recruiter account. Job Seekers can raise an account-deletion request with a proper reason; Admin reviews the request before deletion. For access, correction, privacy or deletion help, raise a ticket through AI Assistant or email akhildivakara@gmail.com.

Children

MR Vacancies Hub is intended for users who are 18 years or older and are seeking or offering employment.
