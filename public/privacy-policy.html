Privacy Policy — MR Vacancies Hub

Last updated: 20 October 2026 • Managed by M Divakar Reddy

MR Vacancies Hub helps Medical Representatives find verified pharma vacancies and helps Recruiters discover suitable candidates while protecting personal contact details.

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, mobile number and email ID. You stay logged in on your device until you choose to log out. After logout, your mobile number is used to find your existing account.

Job Seeker profile information

Job Seekers provide job-related details such as qualification, desired role, experience, current company and role, current and expected package, location/HQ, joining availability, two-wheeler availability and skills. Freshers provide qualification and passing year instead of current-company details.

Recruiters can see: an anonymous candidate code; Fresher/Experienced status; desired role; qualification and, for Freshers, passing year; total experience; current company, current role and current package for experienced candidates; expected package; state, city, current HQ and preferred HQ; notice period or joining availability; two-wheeler availability; and skills entered by the Job Seeker.

Recruiters cannot see: a Job Seeker's name, mobile number or email ID. A Recruiter may request contact through the Admin, and contact details should be shared only with the candidate's consent. Job Seekers can review the exact anonymous view at any time from My Job Profile → What Recruiters See.

Recruiter and vacancy information

Recruiters provide account and vacancy details. Vacancies are reviewed by the Admin before they are published. Recruiter contact details in job posts may be masked until a Job Seeker unlocks them under the app's plan rules.

How information is used

  • To operate Job Seeker and Recruiter accounts.
  • To show relevant vacancies and anonymous candidate profiles.
  • To process applications, saved jobs, subscriptions and support requests.
  • To prevent spam, misuse and unauthorized account activity.
  • To show plan-based advertisements, including sponsored detail reveals on the Free plan and a sponsored Apply Now advertisement before an application is submitted.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, such as secure hosting, database, advertising, payment and AI-assistant services.

Account deletion

Recruiters can delete their Recruiter account from the app. Job Seekers can raise an account-deletion request with a proper reason from their account; the Admin reviews the request before deletion. You may also contact support@mrvacancieshub.in.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect account information. Please keep your device and account access secure and log out on shared devices.

Children

MR Vacancies Hub is intended for adult job seekers and recruiters.
